# Empower Humanity AI - Reality Model v0.1

**Product:** Empower Humanity AI  
**Document Type:** Product Reality Model  
**Status:** Foundational target architecture  
**Scope:** Persistent human-facing Co-Intelligence experience  
**External Systems:** Generative Reality Systems, Construct Context, Ensolam, Cognition Systems, Intelligence Cloud, Complete Systems AI, AI Native Substrate, AI Native Runtime

---

# 1. Purpose and Boundary

This Reality Model defines the realities Empower Humanity AI owns or presents in the relationship between a human and their persistent Co-Intelligence. It deliberately does not redefine the full model-routing ontology of AI Native Substrate or the execution, capability, and governance ontology of AI Native Runtime.

Empower Humanity AI owns the coherent human-facing experience. It understands and collaborates with the human through ecosystem identity, reality, context, cognition, intelligence, and execution systems.

```text
Human
	-> Empower Humanity AI
	-> Co-Intelligence / Cognition
	-> Reality + Context
	-> AI Native Substrate
	-> AI Native Runtime
	-> Devices, applications, resources, workers, models, and services
```

The model describes target architecture, not a claim that every integration or Runtime capability is already implemented.

# 2. Ownership Model

## 2.1 Empower Humanity-Owned Realities

Empower Humanity owns the product-facing interpretation and presentation of:

* Human and Co-Intelligence relationship,
* conversations and interactions,
* goals, intentions, plans, decisions, commitments, and responsibilities,
* user-facing tasks and work items,
* autonomy configuration and governance UX,
* notifications, activity summaries, explanations, and cross-device continuity,
* human-facing reality awareness, preferences, corrections, and personalization.

## 2.2 Referenced Ecosystem Realities

The following are referenced integration objects. Their source systems remain authoritative:

| Reality | Authoritative owner | Empower Humanity role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Being identity and continuity | Ensolam | Presents the user's Co-Intelligence relationship |
| GRL and formal reality representation | Generative Reality Systems | Consumes and renders useful reality understanding |
| Context packages and lifecycle | Construct Context | Supplies relevant context under applicable boundaries |
| Cognitive mechanisms | Cognition Systems | Uses outcomes in Co-Intelligence behavior |
| Specialized intelligence | Intelligence Cloud / Complete Systems AI | Incorporates domain reasoning and system understanding |
| AI interaction, model route, intelligence invocation, and placement | AI Native Substrate | Requests and receives orchestrated intelligence |
| Runtime Node, Runtime Session, Capability, Permission, Approval Request, Execution Job, Result, Resource, Artifact, and Runtime Event | AI Native Runtime | References enough provenance for human understanding and governance |

Context is not authority. Knowing about a resource does not grant permission to access or change it.

# 3. Primary Entities

## 3.1 Human

The Human is the person in the Co-Intelligence relationship. A Human can establish goals, provide context, grant or revoke authority, make decisions, inspect activity, pause work, and exercise applicable final authority.

## 3.2 Primary Co-Intelligence

The Primary Co-Intelligence is the one persistent AI relationship a user experiences across Empower Humanity surfaces. It has a user-facing identity, relationship history, current understanding, responsibilities, initiative preferences, and attributable action history. It is not identical to a specific model, device, agent, or Runtime Node.

## 3.3 Relationship

The Relationship captures the durable collaboration between Human and Co-Intelligence, including communication preferences, corrections, trust boundaries, delegated responsibilities, shared history, and applicable autonomy settings.

## 3.4 Reality State and Context

Reality State is Empower Humanity's user-facing view of relevant people, goals, systems, commitments, preferences, constraints, events, opportunities, and concerns. Context is the relevant bounded information assembled for a current interaction or cognitive operation. Formal representations and context lifecycle are supplied by the specialized ecosystem systems named above.

## 3.5 Goal, Intention, Plan, Decision, Commitment, and Task

These are human-comprehensible objects through which the Co-Intelligence organizes collaboration:

* **Goal:** a desired outcome over a horizon.
* **Intention:** what the Human or Co-Intelligence currently seeks to accomplish.
* **Plan:** an adaptable proposed path toward a goal.
* **Decision:** a recorded choice and its rationale, confidence, and consequences.
* **Commitment:** an accepted responsibility, promise, or constraint.
* **Task / Work Item:** a unit of visible work that may reference one or more external execution jobs.

## 3.6 Autonomy Configuration

Autonomy Configuration expresses the Human's preferred initiative and review posture. It may include approval requirements, notification preferences, delegated responsibility boundaries, and revocation rules. It is a product-facing policy preference; Runtime remains authoritative for evaluating effective capability, resource, application, device, task, session, and risk scopes.

## 3.7 Interaction, Notification, and Activity Summary

An Interaction is a bounded exchange involving the Human and Co-Intelligence. A Notification requests or informs attention. An Activity Summary is a concise, human-readable representation of meaningful reasoning, progress, execution, result, or change. These objects make infrastructure understandable without exposing internal chain-of-thought or raw execution detail by default.

# 4. Execution Reference Model

Empower Humanity needs sufficient referenced execution provenance to explain consequential actions without becoming the system of record for Runtime execution.

```text
Human
	-> Empower Humanity Application
	-> Primary Co-Intelligence / AI Being
	-> Optional Agent
	-> Goal or Work Item
	-> Execution Request
	-> Runtime Node
	-> Capability + Resource
	-> Execution Job
	-> Result / Artifact / Resource Change
	-> Activity Summary and Reality Update
```

For each meaningful referenced execution, Empower Humanity should be able to present, when authorized:

* who or what initiated it,
* the associated goal or Work Item,
* execution location and Runtime Node,
* requested capability and relevant resource scope,
* approval or policy outcome where meaningful,
* current status and result,
* artifacts or material resource changes,
* timestamps and a link to the appropriate execution history.

The Runtime owns the detailed audit record, policy decision, approval state, provider selection, and execution evidence.

---

# 5. Runtime Node and Dedicated Compute Reference

A **Runtime Node** is an externally owned AI Native Runtime execution environment available to the user or organization. Empower Humanity may display its human-readable name, availability, capabilities, health, locality, and allowed relationship to work.

A **Dedicated Compute Node** is a Runtime Node configured for persistent or high-capacity AI work. It may provide authorized persistent storage, CPU/GPU/NPU, local models, applications, browsers, developer tools, workers, schedules, and local workspaces. It uses the same Runtime governance model as any other node. The Human retains owner/root authority; neither the Co-Intelligence nor a subordinate agent receives unrestricted authority by virtue of node designation.

# 6. Core Co-Intelligence Loop

The relationship is persistent and iterative, not a chatbot plus tools.

```text
Human Intent
	-> Co-Intelligence Interpretation
	-> Reality + Context Assembly
	-> Cognition / Reasoning
	-> Intelligence Selection + Interaction
	-> Execution Planning / Placement
	-> Governance / Permission Evaluation
	-> AI Native Runtime Execution
	-> Observation / Execution Result
	-> Reality + Context Update
	-> Continued Reasoning / Human Communication
```

In compact form: **Intent -> Context -> Cognition -> Intelligence -> Execution -> Observation -> Reality Update.** The loop may stop for human input, continue after an execution result, or create an Approval Request before execution can proceed.

---

# 7. Governance and Authority

Human authority is foundational. Empower Humanity provides clear UX for:

* autonomy policy and initiative level,
* capability, resource, device, application, task, and session scopes,
* pending approvals and permission decisions,
* risk explanation and material action visibility,
* execution history, artifacts, and audit references,
* pause, cancel, revocation, emergency stop, and human override.

AI identity does not automatically equal execution authority. Subordinate agents do not automatically inherit unrestricted authority from a Co-Intelligence. Meaningful execution must remain observable and attributable.

---

# 8. Persistence, Locality, and Offline Direction

Co-Intelligence persistence means durable identity, relationship, goals, responsibilities, reality, context, and continuity across interfaces. Runtime persistence means Runtime services, jobs, workers, node connectivity, local processes, queues, and background execution. They support one another but are distinct.

Some locally durable operation is an architectural direction: a Runtime Node may execute approved local filesystem, application, worker, MCP, or local-model work using valid cached authority where the Runtime supports it. This model does not claim full offline Co-Intelligence parity.

---

# 9. Invariants

1. Empower Humanity owns the relationship and human-facing coherence.
2. AI Native Substrate owns intelligence interaction and orchestration infrastructure.
3. AI Native Runtime governs and executes authorized actions.
4. Humans retain applicable ultimate authority.
5. Intelligence and execution are separate concerns.
6. Applications consume shared Runtime interfaces rather than private runtimes.
7. Capabilities are preferred over raw machine primitives.
8. Infrastructure remains largely invisible while meaningful actions remain explainable.
